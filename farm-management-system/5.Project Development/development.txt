SALESFORCE DEV ORG:
A Salesforce Developer Org was created and configured.

CUSTOM OBJECTS:
Farm | Farm Name, Farm Type, Soil Quality, Irrigation Type, Location, Size, Farmer
Crop | Crop Name, Planting Date, Harvest Date, Farm, Actual Yield, Acres Harvested
Farmer | Farmer Name, Contact Number, State, District, Village
Buyer | Buyer Name, Contact, Email, Address, Buyer Type

TABS & APP:
Custom tabs for Farm, Crop, Farmer, Buyer. Lightning App named Farm Management System.

FIELDS & RELATIONSHIPS:
Picklists, text, phone, email, date, currency, number and formula fields. Farm-Farmer and Crop-Farm relationships. State-District dependency.

PROFILES, ROLES & SHARING:
Custom profiles Farm Manager and Sales Department. Farm Manager role and users. Sharing rules for Read/Write access.

REPORTS & DASHBOARD:
Report: Farmers with Farms - grouping by Farmer Name.
Dashboard: Farmers with Farms using Gauge/Chart.

DEMO STEPS:
Open App Launcher -> Search Farm Management System -> Open Farm/Crop/Farmer/Buyer tab -> Open Reports -> Open Dashboards -> Verify records and chart.
